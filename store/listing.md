**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Keep a simple, private record of your blood pressure and pulse.

• Log systolic, diastolic, pulse, date, time and notes in seconds
• Readings are automatically grouped by AHA/ACC category:
  Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2 and Hypertensive Crisis
• See your latest reading, averages, highest and lowest at a glance
• Trend charts for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or all time
• Export a PDF report to share with your doctor
• Light and dark themes

Private by design
Your readings never leave your phone unless you export them yourself.
No account, no ads, no analytics, no internet permission.

Medical disclaimer
BP Tracker is a personal log. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any
condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always
consult your healthcare provider.
```
