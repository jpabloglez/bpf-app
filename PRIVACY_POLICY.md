# Privacy Policy — BP Tracker

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

BP Tracker ("the app") is a personal blood pressure log for Android.

## What the app stores

The readings you enter (systolic and diastolic pressure, pulse, date and time,
and optional notes) are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private
storage. Other apps cannot read it.

## What the app collects or shares

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no
crash reporting, and it does not request internet access. The developer
never receives your data.

## Exporting reports

When you choose **Export PDF report**, the app creates a PDF of your readings
and opens the Android share sheet. The PDF goes only to the destination you
pick (for example email or a messaging app), and that destination's own
privacy policy then applies.

## Backups

Readings are excluded from Google cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, they
move to a new phone when you use Android's device-to-device transfer. To keep
your own copy, export a PDF report.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual readings in the app. Uninstalling the app permanently
deletes all of its data from the device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Medical disclaimer

BP Tracker does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always
consult a qualified healthcare provider about your blood pressure.
